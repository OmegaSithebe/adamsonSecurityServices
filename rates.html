Choose Your Plan

Two flexible plans designed to protect what matters — whether you're safeguarding a business or your family home.

Business Plan

For offices, retail, warehouses and industrial sites.

  • Trained on-site security officers (armed or unarmed)
  • Access control and visitor management
  • Routine patrols and incident reporting
  • 24/7 monitoring and rapid response coordination
  • Optional CCTV, alarm and perimeter integration

Home Plan

For residential properties, estates and complexes.

  • Residential armed and unarmed officers
